Custom Oil Pressing Service

Get Your Own Mustard Seed Pressed at Our Mill

If you have your own mustard seed from your field, bring it to Apna Oil Mill and we will press it into pure oil at our mill on a job-work basis. You receive your own oil and oil cake back — charges are calculated by the weight of the seed.

Locally, this service is known as Sarso Pilai.

Step 1

Bring your seed to the mill, or arrange pickup by contacting us.

Step 2

Seed is weighed in front of you before pressing.

Step 3

Pressing charge is calculated by weight — confirm the current rate with us directly.

Step 4

Take home your own pure oil and oil cake, pressed the same day where possible.

This service and our delivery are currently available only within Bulandshahr district. We are unable to service other locations at this time.
